# Terminal facts visual review — final v2

This is a read-only review of the final `terminal-facts-qualified-v2-20260923` gallery against `terminal-facts-baseline-20260923`. The intermediate v1 gallery was used only to prepare contact sheets. SHA-256 comparison of every one of the 516 PNGs shows that v1 and final v2 captures are byte-identical. I also opened final v2 originals for scene 144 in dark 480 DIP/96 DPI and high contrast 480 DIP/144 DPI directly.

The final comparison found 34 pixel-changed images among the 508 common names and no dimension changes. The affected common scenes were 19 (two 480-DIP monitor captures) and 21, 22, 23, 65, 66, 67, 114, 115 (each at 480/760 DIP on both 144/96-DPI displays). The eight candidate-only scene-144 images cover dark/high-contrast × 480/760 DIP × both displays. I inspected all 42 changed/new originals through the 11 analysis contact sheets in `terminal-facts-review-sheets`; byte identity binds those observations to final v2. The PNGs themselves remain in the archived runner gallery. A contact sheet is an analysis aid, not an application capture.

In these final v2 captures the new Source, Publication and Verification paragraphs remain within their task cards. At 480 DIP, the three-axis scene 144 wraps over several full lines above warnings and paths; the progress bar and Close/action row remain separated below. The high-contrast and 96-DPI versions show the same ordering and readable count clauses. The changed Move and verification scenes likewise place the new fact line between the summary and the existing warning/path rows without visible overlap. I found no new visible clipping or action collision in these 42 frames. Existing path ellipses are still present and are not a regression in this slice.

The other 474 common frames are byte-identical to baseline; this visual review makes no claim about uncaptured cache/notification behavior. The v2 raw trace separately records 24 same-HWND transitions, two stable repaints, and two settings refresh observations (`rebuilds=1`, `repeats=0`, `stable=true` at each native DPI). That trace is behavioral evidence, not a substitute for interactive/UIA qualification.
