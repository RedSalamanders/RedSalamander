# VLC capture route and black-layer failures

The generated AVI decoded with real positive duration/time in both rejected
attempts. The first fixture sent Space to the parent, while the production
playback shortcut belongs to the video child. An initial thread explanation
was incorrect: the standalone viewer uses the test owner UI thread. Moving
pause to the video child passed real pause and clock stability, but native WGC
frames still contained a black video area and failed the color witness. The
initial wrong-thread negative-control assumption was invalid too.

No rejected frame is published. Original observations, receipts and complete
failed runs have verified private archives. The final fixture captures through
a joined MTA worker while the owner UI pumps, uses 60 generated frames at 5 fps,
and pins software decoding with wingdi output. Corrected native validation
passes positive video pixels, real pause and stable time, actual foreign-thread
client-probe rejection and absent-color rejection. Complete successful frames
and identities are in the supplemental validation archive.

These combined repairs do not isolate the original black-layer cause between
presentation timing and output-module behavior. No playback state or screenshot
pixels are injected, and no global keyboard/mouse input is used. This qualifies
the identified software-output capture scenario, not every GPU/output module.
